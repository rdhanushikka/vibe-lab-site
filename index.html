Vibe coding hours

A biweekly creative coding sprint. We bring ideas, we vote, we build. Two hours, one challenge, and everyone leaves with something that runs.

Vote on the next challenge

We bring ideas

Anyone in the WhatsApp group can pitch a challenge before the session. Half-formed is fine.

We vote

The group votes on the pitches through the mailing list and WhatsApp. The winner becomes the sprint.

We build

Show up, pair up, and prototype with AI tools. Demos at the end, no polish required.
